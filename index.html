💰 Levy continues to rise

The General Fund levy increases from about $1.30 million in 2025 to $1.38 million in 2026 and $1.43 million in the adopted 2027 budget, even while the estimated mill rate declines.

👮 Police wages change direction

The budgeted PD wage line fell from $494,000 in 2025 to $455,306.25 in 2026, then rises to $519,250 in the adopted 2027 budget.

🏗️ Engineering is a major comparison point

The adopted 2026 budget began with $160,000 for General Fund engineering professional fees, but the council later amended that line to $332,000. The adopted 2027 General Fund line is $46,000, with additional engineering amounts appearing in other funds.

🌾 Prairie Dog Fund structure changes

The 2025 and 2026 budgets each showed a $1,997,556.92 infrastructure-project appropriation. The adopted 2027 Prairie Dog Fund worksheet instead shows no infrastructure-project expenditure and lists a $123,632.96 transfer out.

August budget and project updates

The August 6 minutes confirm approval of the $52,885.47 Police Ford Interceptor purchase, the $28,468.32 Insurance Reserve Fund transfer and closure of Fund 211, the Toolcat repair/sale/replacement plan, and the 2027 preliminary budget. On August 20, the wastewater project was reported near the 90% design stage with an estimated project cost of about $30 million, a utility delinquency ordinance advanced through first reading, and additional City Center property negotiations were reported as moving toward paperwork for future council consideration. The City's 2027 Annual Budget document now states that the final budget was adopted September 17, 2026.

Why this timeline matters

Personnel, engineering, capital, fleet, and fund-transfer decisions can affect the city's broader financial plan. The timeline separates verified approvals from proposals and items whose final outcome still needs to be confirmed.

About the figures: The “known dollar actions” figure combines different kinds of financial activity, including a pension transfer, capital purchases, contracts, an over-budget amount, a fund transfer, and increased budget authority. It should not be described as a total of new spending.
